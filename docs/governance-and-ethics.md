# Governance and Ethics

The **AI Water Infrastructure Leak and Contamination Digital Twin** is an independent synthetic research prototype. It is designed for learning, simulation, planning-support experiments, robustness testing, and responsible AI workflow design for water infrastructure monitoring.

## Intended use

Acceptable uses include:

- Studying leak-like and contamination-like anomaly detection with synthetic telemetry.
- Teaching water-infrastructure digital twins and cyber-physical system monitoring.
- Prototyping response-priority ranking for engineering review.
- Testing robustness under missing, noisy, or drifting sensor values.
- Building reproducible reports, figures, and audit trails.

## Non-intended use

The project is not intended for:

- Drinking-water safety certification.
- Public-health advice or boil-water advisory decisions.
- SCADA, PLC, pump, valve, tank, or utility-network control.
- Emergency response automation.
- Regulatory reporting or compliance certification.
- Real-world contamination claims without certified lab confirmation.
- Replacing utility engineers, public-health authorities, regulators, or certified laboratories.

## Water-safety principle

Water infrastructure decisions can affect public health, trust, service continuity, and community safety. Any real-world deployment requires calibrated field instruments, chain-of-custody procedures, certified laboratory sampling, expert review, uncertainty communication, and formal governance.

## Data governance

This repository ships fictional synthetic zones, pipes, pumps, tanks, sensors, and telemetry. No real utility data, customer data, geolocation traces, infrastructure diagrams, SCADA logs, or public-health records are included.

Real data integration would require privacy review, security review, licensing, data minimization, retention controls, operational approvals, and incident-response procedures.

## AI-control boundary

The detection and priority scores are review prompts. They should not trigger autonomous public alerts, physical infrastructure actions, emergency response, or regulatory claims.

## Responsible statement

This lab supports research and education. It should never be used as the sole basis for public-health communication, water-safety decisions, infrastructure operation, emergency response, or real contamination allegations.
